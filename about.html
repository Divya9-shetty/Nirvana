About Nirvana

Nirvana is a mental health support platform designed to help users monitor and improve their mental well-being. It provides tools such as self-assessment questionnaires, a personal diary, and a chatbot to provide guidance, coping strategies, and resources for conditions like anxiety, PTSD, ADHD, depression, bipolar disorder, and schizophrenia spectrum disorder.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make mental health support accessible, intuitive, and personalized. Nirvana encourages self-awareness, reflection, and proactive mental wellness through simple, user-friendly tools.
